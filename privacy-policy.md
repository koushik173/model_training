# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 27, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Oihik ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you interact with our Facebook Page through Messenger.

## 2. Information We Collect

When you send a message to our Facebook Page, our application may receive and process:

- Your Facebook user identifier provided by Meta.
- The text and content of messages you send.
- Message timestamps and relevant webhook event information.
- Information necessary to respond to your messages and maintain the service.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through Messenger. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information through our chatbot.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we receive to:

- Receive and respond to Messenger messages.
- Generate automated responses using AI services, if enabled.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve our chatbot.
- Monitor the operation and security of our service.
- Protect the service against misuse and comply with applicable legal requirements.

## 4. AI Processing and Third-Party Services

Oihik may use third-party AI service providers to generate automated responses. When an external AI provider is used, message content may be sent to that provider for processing and response generation, subject to the provider's applicable privacy terms and data-handling practices.

We do not claim that messages are processed exclusively on our own server when external AI services are involved. We will update this policy if our AI providers or data-processing practices change materially.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

Our application may temporarily process message content and Facebook user identifiers on our server to deliver chatbot functionality.

Information contained in server logs, files, or databases, if any, may be retained for troubleshooting, security, and operational purposes. We aim to retain personal information only for as long as necessary for these purposes and applicable legal obligations, and to delete it when it is no longer needed.

We do not promise a fixed deletion period because retention depends on the information stored and the actual operation of the service.

## 6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by Meta, AI service providers, hosting providers, and other technical service providers when necessary to operate the chatbot. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security of our service and its users.

Meta and other third-party providers process information under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by our application against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You may stop interacting with our Facebook Page at any time.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your interactions, contact us at **oihik.bd@gmail.com**. Please describe your request without including passwords or other sensitive information.

We will review and respond to requests as required by applicable law. Some information may need to be retained where legally required or necessary for legitimate security purposes.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where doing so would violate applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us so we can review the request.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our service, technology, or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
